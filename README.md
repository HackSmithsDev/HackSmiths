# 🚀 About HackSmiths

HackSmiths is a student-led technology organization focused on building impactful software, participating in hackathons, contributing to open-source, developing AI-powered solutions, creating embedded systems, and delivering professional digital products.

Our objective is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry-level development by encouraging innovation, collaboration, research, and practical engineering.

Whether it's developing modern web platforms, intelligent AI applications, mobile apps, autonomous drones, embedded systems, or robotics, HackSmiths aims to transform ideas into real-world solutions.

---

# 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to create a collaborative ecosystem where passionate developers, designers, engineers, and innovators work together to solve real-world problems using modern technologies.

We believe in:

- Building meaningful products
- Learning through practical experience
- Contributing to open source
- Participating in hackathons
- Delivering professional software solutions
- Growing together as engineers

---

# 🔭 Our Vision

To become one of India's leading student-driven technology organizations by fostering innovation, technical excellence, and collaborative software development.

Our long-term vision is to build products that positively impact industries while empowering students to become future technology leaders.
